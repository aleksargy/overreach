# Decisions

What was decided and why, newest at the bottom. Add a row when something changes direction.

| When   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 24 Oct | Use the Garmin Personal API, with the key in a Netlify Function and never in the browser.                                                                                                                                                          | It's free and self-serve, and a key in browser code would expose Alex's health data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 24 Oct | The first milestone is "balanced", with football up front: the match report (score, goals, assists, how it felt, then a rating). Ghost replays come later.                                                                                         | Alex's pick from the concept pitch.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 24 Oct | Build from scratch. TrainLog (`aleksargy/fitness`) is retired and none of its data is imported.                                                                                                                                                    | Alex's call.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 24 Oct | Store data in Netlify Blobs instead of Supabase.                                                                                                                                                                                                   | One platform, nothing extra to sign up for.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 24 Oct | Use a fresh repo, `aleksargy/overreach`, with clean history.                                                                                                                                                                                       | Alex created it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 24 Oct | Sync when the app opens (the server calls Garmin at most once every 10 minutes), plus one job at 05:00 UTC, instead of hourly.                                                                                                                     | Alex wanted fewer calls. Hourly made about 120 calls a day, nearly all finding nothing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 24 Oct | Focus on the body and dashboard, a minimal look, deep running analysis and football.                                                                                                                                                               | Alex: "i dont want to spread ourselves too thin just yet".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 24 Oct | Minimal redesign: Geist only, sentence case, colour only for data. Tabs are Today, Run, Football and Body, and the full feed moved under Today as "All activity".                                                                                  | Follows the focus above.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 24 Oct | The Run tab gets the aerobic engine, durability, critical speed with race predictions, and hill, heat and football costs.                                                                                                                          | Proposed to Alex as the analysis Garmin doesn't do.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 25 Oct | The weigh-in takes weight only.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Alex doesn't track measurements or body fat yet.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 25 Oct | Remove "usual opponent" from Settings, and compare 11s and 7s separately.                                                                                                                                                                          | Alex: "doesnt make too much sense to have so much overlap in comparison between the two, some yes but not everything".                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 25 Oct | Lift stays hidden from the tab bar, still reachable at `/lift`.                                                                                                                                                                                    | Alex confirmed. Running and football first.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 25 Oct | Keep "Overreach" as the working name.                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Alex isn't ready to pick; see `roadmap.md` for the shortlist.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 25 Oct | Rewrite the history so aleksargy authors every commit, and add no AI co-author or session trailers from now on.                                                                                                                                    | Every commit was authored by Claude, so GitHub listed only "claude" as a contributor.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 25 Oct | Deploy from GitHub Actions, not Netlify's own git builds.                                                                                                                                                                                          | A deploy happens only after the checks pass, docs-only changes skip it (each production deploy costs credits), and previews ship no functions.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 25 Oct | main takes changes only through pull requests with the `check` job passing; no force pushes or deletion; squash or rebase merges for a straight history.                                                                                           | A solo repo still benefits from CI as the gate, and agents work on branches.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 25 Oct | Copy what Alex types to the server (Blobs key `user`) and bring it back on a new or reset phone.                                                                                                                                                   | Matches, workouts and weigh-ins only lived on the phone; removing the home-screen app would have lost the match history.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 25 Oct | Use varlock for secrets on the Mac; the deployed site keeps them in Netlify.                                                                                                                                                                       | The key stays encrypted on disk, the schema documents every variable for people and agents, and CI checks the code against it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 25 Oct | A sync step does 20 s of work, and no Garmin request runs past 24 s.                                                                                                                                                                               | Netlify's docs give synchronous functions 30 s in one place and 60 s in another, so plan for 30.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 25 Oct | Rebuild readiness around recovery: Garmin's training effect and load decide when a session is still being absorbed, HRV is judged against Garmin's baseline, sleep against Garmin's sleep need, plus Body Battery. No guessed plan in the verdict. | It said "go hard" the day after a 32 km run: it never looked at yesterday, and it guessed the plan. Garmin's recovery time isn't in the Personal API, so this reasons from the same inputs.                                                                                                                                                      |
| 25 Oct | Use Garmin's training load for load, and Garmin's zones for zones.                                                                                                                                                                                 | They're what the watch uses; the app had been working out its own.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 25 Oct | Read the watch RPE and feel for runs and games from the last 30 days, one call each.                                                                                                                                                               | Alex enters them on the watch; older ones aren't worth the extra calls.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 25 Oct | Only ask to rate games from the last 14 days, and let a swipe skip one.                                                                                                                                                                            | Alex: a game from June, last season, isn't one anyone remembers well enough to rate.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 25 Oct | New logo: a chalked pitch with the ball where the touchline opens, generated by `scripts/icons.mjs`.                                                                                                                                               | Alex's design, replacing the three chevrons.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 25 Oct | Race support is a toggle in Settings, off by default: countdown, goal pace, heart rate at goal pace, long runs, taper target.                                                                                                                      | Alex: "i'm not usually prepping for a race"; the marathon on 8 Nov is the exception.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 25 Oct | The Run tab answers plain questions (Fitness, Endurance, Speed, what slows you down) with the meaning first and the method folded away.                                                                                                            | Alex: "i don't get what i'm looking at or what it means exactly".                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 25 Oct | No football-legs card on the Run tab; the model still accounts for runs after a game.                                                                                                                                                              | Alex only jogs easy after a game, and the next quality session is two or three days later.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 26 Oct | Moments are replaced by what went well and badly, in your own words. Each point counts 0.15, a big one 0.3, and the notes move a rating 0.6 at most.                                                                                               | Alex preferred notes to fixed chips. Counting them keeps the notes meaningful; the cap keeps the result and goals in charge.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 26 Oct | Head to head covers every season but only the format you're looking at, and needs two meetings.                                                                                                                                                    | Rivalries span seasons, but an 11s side and a 7s side are different teams.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 26 Oct | The season heatmap frames each ground from all its games and orients each half from the kick-off position, and says so when it can't tell.                                                                                                         | Each game's own frame stretches a winger to fill the pitch, and the GPS alone can't say which end was yours.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 26 Oct | Race reports scale the watch's distance to the race distance and take the finish from the watch.                                                                                                                                                   | Watches measure courses long, so the watch's distance would put the finish line early.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 26 Oct | Ghosts are matched from summaries, then confirmed on the streams already stored in Blobs.                                                                                                                                                          | No Garmin calls, and only real repeats of the same route count.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 26 Oct | Easy is zones 1–3 in the easy-and-hard split.                                                                                                                                                                                                      | On Alex's zones easy runs sit in zone 3 (Garmin's 'aerobic'), the same 145 bpm the Fitness card calls easy.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 26 Oct | Ghosts are matched by place: each run summary keeps a 100 m outline, and two outlines are lined up point by point. Nine tenths of each run within 40 m of the other's route, and no detour over 150 m.                                             | Lining runs up by share of distance failed on real runs: a start a few doors apart or a short extra loop put the rest hundreds of metres out, so 4 of 58 true repeats passed, and the card offered ghosts the screen then didn't find. The outline adds about 100 KB a year to the data download and lets the card know before any stream loads. |
